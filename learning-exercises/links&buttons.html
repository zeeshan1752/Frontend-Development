Try it: Use Tab to focus a link or button, then adjust its focus style.

Detailed explanation: Styling links and buttons

Links navigate to a destination; buttons perform an action. CSS pseudo-classes let a design respond to states such as hover, keyboard focus, visited, or active. A visible focus indicator is essential for people navigating with a keyboard.

Example: Use :hover for a pointer state and :focus-visible for keyboard focus. An anchor should have an href; a button should have an appropriate type.

Common pitfall: Do not use an anchor as a button when there is no destination, or a button as a link. Keep hover and focus states visually clear and do not rely on color alone.

Styling links and buttons

Links take people to another location. Buttons perform an action. Their hover, focus, and active states help show how they respond.
