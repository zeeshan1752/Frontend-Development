Detailed explanation: Links and images

An anchor creates a link, and its href names the destination. Relative paths point to nearby files; full URLs point to other sites. An image's alt text communicates its useful content when the image cannot be seen or loaded. Decorative images can use an empty alt value.

Example: A link to tutorial.html opens another local lesson. A description such as alt='Simple illustrated profile' tells the reader what the sample image depicts.

Common pitfall: Avoid link text such as click here when the destination is not clear. Do not use a filename as alt text; describe the image's purpose or leave alt empty if it is purely decorative.
