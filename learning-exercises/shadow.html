What you'll learn: How box shadows add depth to elements and text shadows emphasize text.

Try it: Adjust the shadow blur and offset to make the effect softer or stronger.

Detailed explanation: Box shadows and text shadows

A box shadow draws a shadow around an element's rectangular box. Its values can include horizontal offset, vertical offset, blur, spread, color, and an optional inset keyword. A text shadow applies similar offsets and blur to glyphs.

Example: A subtle shadow such as 0 4px 14px rgb(15 23 42 / 12%) adds depth without overpowering the card. Increase blur for a softer edge.

Common pitfall: A later box-shadow declaration replaces an earlier one unless both shadows are combined in one comma-separated declaration. Keep contrast strong enough for text.

Box shadows and text shadows

box-shadow draws a shadow around a box. text-shadow draws one behind the letters.

Card with a soft shadow

Change the horizontal offset, vertical offset, blur, or color in the style block. A larger blur makes the shadow softer.
