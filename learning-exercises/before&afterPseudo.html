What you'll learn: How ::before and ::after add decorative content without extra HTML elements.

Try it: Add a decorative marker with a pseudo-element and keep meaningful text in the HTML.

Detailed explanation: Before and after pseudo-elements

The ::before and ::after pseudo-elements create generated content attached to an element's content box. They require a content declaration and are commonly used for visual decoration, markers, or small shapes without adding extra markup.

Example: A heading can use ::before and ::after to add decorative stars around its text. The actual heading remains ordinary HTML.

Common pitfall: Keep essential information out of generated content because assistive technologies may not expose it consistently. Use real HTML for meaningful words and controls.
